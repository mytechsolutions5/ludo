📖 Rules of Ludo

🎲
Roll a 6 to Exit Base: Roll a 6 to bring a piece out of your yard onto your starting square. Rolling a 6 also awards an extra roll!
⭐
Safe Spots: Squares marked with Stars and Starting squares are Safe Zones. Tokens on these squares cannot be captured!
💥
Capturing Opponents: Landing on an opponent's token in an unsafe cell sends it back to its yard and gives you a bonus roll!
⚠️
Three Sixes Rule: Rolling three 6s in a row forfeits that turn and passes play to the next player.
🏆
Winning: The first player to safely navigate all 4 tokens around the board and into the Center Home wins!
